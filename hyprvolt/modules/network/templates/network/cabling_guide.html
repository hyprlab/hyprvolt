Suggest cables

The cables are worked out from what the steps before recorded. The modem goes to the router or firewall, that to the core switch (the switch nearest it), and every other switch to the core. A pair of MoCA adapters is joined over the coax, and a wireless bridge's near end is plugged in. Every other device goes to the nearest switch by where each is: same rack, then same room, then same building. Switches with their ports recorded get their free ports in order.

Wireless extenders, patch panels and peripherals are left out, and so is anything that already has a cable. You check every cable before any is added.
