{# The Hypervisor section in a server's form: Runs a hypervisor, ticked, makes the hypervisor record running on it, with its platform and the address it is managed at; unticked deletes it, asked first. #}
A hypervisor record of the same name, running on this server, in Hypervisors. Its management address is where its web interface is; the server's own IP addresses are then its iDRAC, iLO or IPMI.
