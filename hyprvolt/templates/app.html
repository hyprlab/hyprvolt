Keep them safe

Backups are kept in {{ backup_folder }}. Unless BACKUP_DIR puts them on another disk, they are on the same disk as the data: download them now and then, and keep them somewhere else.

Secrets are in a backup, encrypted, but their key is not, on purpose. Download the key once from Settings > Secrets and keep it apart from the backups; after restoring on a new server, put it back there.
